Patent Landscape Report:

Search in different databases using keyword w.r.to product and IUPAC names and identified relevant patents/patent applications all over the world and prepared synthetic schemes disclosed in that relevant patents and their legal status also we will provide. This is very exhaustive search and take minimum 20-25 days. Because we have to read the patents and identified the route of synthesis. Further, The biggest task to this report is Legal status of each patent.

IP clearance report:

This is the report, it is the legally verified report. Because whatever, route of synthesis provided by the customer, we will check whether the proposed route is cleared/not infringed w.r.to particularly geography proposed by the customer. Country specific non-infringement opinion will give clearance. So that, there is no infringement issue in future to launch the product in that country.

Freedom to Operate / Infringement Analysis / Clearance Report:

This is also some what clear to market the market. Here, we will search all the patents and identified reference in that particular country and we will check whether the proposed process is free to operate or not. Simply, this report is called as claim based analysis. Only valid patents/application will consider for this analysis. Generally, This type of analysis done before the launch of the product in the market.

Patentability Report:

This type of report we made only when the patent application was filed. Before filing any patent application, it is mandatory to check, whether the proposed process is novel, inventive or industrial applicability

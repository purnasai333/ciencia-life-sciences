About Ciencia

Built by researchers for the life-sciences community

Ciencia Life Sciences is an integrated life-sciences research company offering research training, services and innovative solutions. The organisation was started by PhD holders with a strong research background after observing the need for an integrated service provider for fast-growing life-sciences and biotech companies and academics.

The motto is to provide good research training by qualified professionals, together with integrated consulting and outsourcing of research.

Ciencia is a bioinformatics training organisation in Hyderabad, providing services and training in bioinformatics and cheminformatics. Advanced trainings, short courses, summer projects and Bio-IT courses (BioPerl, Python) are offered in classroom and online formats.

Training

Professional life-sciences training with practical exposure

Training is designed to prepare students and professionals as researchers, with the knowledge needed to implement concepts in their field. Ciencia also provides hands-on experience with instruments and practical exposure to protocol methodologies.

Classroom and online options are available. Summer training, short courses, internships and Bio-IT courses sit alongside longer bioinformatics and cheminformatics programmes.

Research services

Ciencia supports researchers by analysing data from their projects and by helping write projects for funding agencies and publications. The data-analysis group assists at every step of a research project. Ciencia also supports clinical trials and pharma research using cheminformatics tools such as docking, virtual screening and pharmacophore generation.

Confidentiality is maintained. Information provided is securely held and is not shared with other persons or companies.

Research services

Scientific writing

Writing is the principal means of communicating research findings. Ciencia offers guidance on scientific articles and research projects for scholars and researchers at all stages of life-sciences research, including data analysis, statistics and bioinformatics.

Services include journal publication writing, government funding/grant proposals (DST, DBT) and thesis writing assistance.

Founder

Dr. Sabitha Yadam

Dr. Sabitha Yadam is CEO and Research Head at Ciencia Labs LLP, Hyderabad, with extensive academic and research experience. She has worked as Research Head at Ciencia Life Sciences, Assistant Professor at JNIAS, and Research Associate at institutions including Indo-American Cancer Hospital and Mahavir Hospital.

Her expertise spans drug design, cheminformatics, pharmacogenomics, molecular biology, probiotics, nanotechnology and AI-driven drug discovery. She has contributed through patents, publications and conference presentations, has guided over 500 students, and has organised national conferences and workshops.

She holds a Ph.D. in Biotechnology from JNTU, Hyderabad, with research focused on molecular mechanisms and oxidative stress in smokers with head and neck squamous cell carcinoma. Bioinformatics skills include molecular docking, QSAR studies and computational drug design.

What is PureAlert-C?

Designed as a practical preliminary screening device

PureAlert is a water-testing device supplied in a sealed plastic bottle containing approximately 3 mL of coloured liquid reagent, together with product packaging and instructions for use.

The device is designed to provide a visible response when a water sample is tested under the specified conditions. It is intended for preliminary water-quality screening and should be interpreted as a practical screening aid rather than a definitive diagnostic or universal drinking-water safety test.

PureAlert-C is best understood as a simple, easy-to-use tool for initial indication and awareness, particularly where routine screening may help identify when more detailed microbiological investigation is needed.

Why coliform screening matters

Indicator-based water monitoring

Coliform bacteria are commonly used as indicators in water-quality monitoring. Their detection can indicate the need for additional microbiological investigation and may help prioritise further testing in a water-safety workflow.

It is important to remember that detection of coliform bacteria does not automatically prove faecal contamination, and a negative PureAlert result does not prove that water is safe to drink.

Responsible scientific message

PureAlert-C is intended for preliminary screening and does not replace comprehensive drinking-water testing.

Know the limits

What PureAlert-C does not test

PureAlert is intended for preliminary microbiological screening. It does not replace comprehensive water-quality testing and should not be presented as a method for detecting:

  • Chemical contaminants
  • Heavy metals
  • Every type of microorganism
  • Definitive drinking-water safety

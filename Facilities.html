Laboratory facilities

Doctoral teaching, laboratory equipment and computational resources for research, training and consultancy.

Research students benefit from doctoral faculty teaching, current research resources and equipment, dedicated staff, and information technology including bioinformatics and cheminformatics for research analysis.

Ciencia admits academically able and motivated students and aims to provide strong teaching, resources and facilities. Facilities are continually updated across life-sciences subject areas. A number of facilities are also available for commercial testing and consultancy.

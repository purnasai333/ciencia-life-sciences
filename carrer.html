Careers

Systematic, equal recruitment for people with relevant life-sciences qualifications.

To build on current success and continuous development, Ciencia welcomes speculative enquiries and applications from people with the right attitude, appropriate experience and/or a similar industrial background, and relevant qualifications with a master’s degree in life sciences.

Through its policies and procedures, Ciencia is committed to ensuring that recruitment, selection, promotion and development of employees is conducted in a manner that is systematic, effective and promotes equality and opportunity for all.

No specific open vacancy list is published on the current site. Applications are received speculatively.

How to apply

Research Services

Ciencia supports Researches by Intelligently analyzing data from their projects, support to writing Projects to submit any funding agencies and publications. Our data analysis group are able to help move your research project forward in a number of ways, and we have the experience to assist you at every step of the process. We will provide regular progress reports and after the analysis we will provide you with all output files that we have generated.

We support clinical trials and Pharma research by applying chemiinformatics tools like docking, virtual screening and Pharmacogenarations, to help drive research forward. There is a huge choice of bioinformatics softwares, and it can often be difficult to choose the tools that will be most suitable for your data. We can help you in this to design experiments and discuss the most effective ways to analyse your data. If you want to carry out your own bioinformatics analysis but have some querys, we will help troubleshoot your work as needed.

We maintaining the confidentiality of those who use our services and will keep all information confidentially. We are committed to improve your confidence by safeguarding your personal information. All the information you provide to us is securely maintained and is kept strictly confidential. We do not, and will not, share your work with any other persons and companies.

Please contact us if you would like to start a discussion about how we can help to support your bioinformatics and chemiinformatics research.

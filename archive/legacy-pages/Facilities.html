Facilities Available

Our research students benefit from Doctrol faculty teaching. The latest research resources and equipment, dedicated staff and information technology including Bioinformatics and Chemiinformatics for research analysis.
We admit the most academically able and motivated students and provide them with inspiring by teaching. We’re confident that we also offer the best resources and facilities to support their learning.
Our dedication to continually updating and improving our facilities in every subject area of the Lifesciences. Indeed, a number of our facilities are also available for use in commercial testing and consultancy.

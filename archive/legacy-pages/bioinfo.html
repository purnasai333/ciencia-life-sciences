Bioinformatics Training

This course is future for all the professionals who want to understand the basics of Bioinformatics and its applications for furthering their career. Cheminformatics and Bioinformatics training in India have profound demand because of high promise in R&D ahead. This Program aims to provide hands-on experience in Biological databases, Genomics tools, Proteomics tools, visualizing tools, molecular modeling, ligand screening, docking and its techniques, ADMET prediction and Computer Aided Drug Designing (CADD).

Topics covered in the courses are

Basics course of Cheminformatics

  • Bioinformatics-an Overview, Definition and History Information Networks Internet in Bioinformatics. Classification of amino Acids, Classification and three Dimensional structures of proteins. Overview of protein structure, Ramachandran Plot, Protein purification, Post translation modification, Protein trafficking.
  • Chemical structure representations : ChemDraw, MarvinSketch, ACD/ChemSketch, jsMol Editor, Marvin molecule editor and viewer, Ketcher, UCSFChimera, Pymol, OpenStructure, OpenBabel, PoseView, Ligplot+
  • DATABASES: Protein Information Resources- Biological Databases, Primary Sequence databases, GSDB (Genome Sequence Database), Secondary Databases Genome Information Resources- DNA sequence Databases - EMBL, DDBJ, Genbank.
    Tools-- Uniprot, NCBI, Zinc database,Prosite, Prints, Blocks, Profiles and Identity. Protein Data bank (PDB), Nucleic Acid Data Bank (NDB), Molecular modeling Data Bank (MMDB), Uniprot, Prosite, SCOP, CATH, KEGG (Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes) databases, Prodom.
  • Gene prediction and functional analysis. Primer designing, ESTs and Gene discovery, Restriction site detection. Tools-- Open Reading Frame Finder, StarORF, Translator, Translate, Virtual Ribosome, Back Translation, CodonW, Primer3, Webcutter, NEBcutter.
  • Matrix. Fold recognition. Sequence analysis: pairewise sequence comparison- sequence queries against biological databases- BLAST and FASTA multifunctional tools for analysis. Pair wise sequence alignment, gaps, gap- penalties, scoring matrices, PAM250, BLOSUM62, local and global sequence alignment, multiple sequence alignment, Tools -- BLAST, FASTA, ClusatlW
  • DNA, RNA Secondary structure predictions. Propensity value. Tools--JPred, PSIPRED, GOR, CentroidFold, IPknot, Mfold, RNAfold
  • PROTEIN STRUCTURE PREDICTION: Homology modeling and Threading. Prediction of protein structure from sequences, functional sites. Tools--Modelar, I-TASSER, Phyre2, Swissmodel, PepSea, PepMAPPER, FindPept.
  • Binding site analysis, Model evaluation, and Model validation. CASTp TRAPP, CAVER, sc-PDB, Pocketome, 3DLigandSite, metaPocket, PockDrug
  • PHYLOGENETIC ANALYSIS : Evolution, elements of phylogeny, methods of phylogenetic analysis, Phylogenetic tree of life, comparison of genetic sequence of organisms, phylogenetic analysis tools- MEGA, Phylip, ClustalW
  • Genetic algorithm, Algorithms in Bioinformatics
  • Docking of small molecules. Autodock vina, Igemdock, HDock

Advanced cheminformatics

  • Molecular Modeling : Introduction, force field, quantum chemistry, Schrödinger equation, potential energy functions, energy minimization, local and global minima, saddle point, grid search, , various approximations; LCAO, HF, semi-empirical calculations; single point calculations, full-geometry optimization methods, ZDO, MNDO, CNDO, NDDO, AM1, PM3, , conformational search, Z-matrix, Energy minimisation, molecular modeling packages. CharmM, Amber etc.tools
  • Molecular Dynamics, Introduction, Newton’s equation of motion, equilibrium point, radial distribution function, pair correlation functions introduction semi- empritical, Molecular MECHANICS and ab intio techniques Simulation techniques potential energy surfaces
  • Pharmacophoric pattern : Structure based PharmacoPhore generations, Ligand based PharmacoPhore generations; Tools: Pharmagist Pharmer, Catalyst, PharmaGist, SwissSimilarity, Blaster, AnchorQuery
  • ADME Properties; QikProp. GastroPlus, VolSurf. ADMEWORKS ModelBuilder, ADMEWORKS Predictor.
  • Quantitative structure activity relationship Use of genetic algorithms and principle component analysis in the QSAR equations
  • Drug Discovery and Computer Aided Drug Designing : Introduction, drug discovery area, pharmacogenetics and pharmacogenomics applications, SNPs, parameters in drug discovery identification of drug target molecules, drug design and its approaches, ligand design methods, docking programs; De novo design
  • Recent advances drug design methodologies, Biomolecular structure, Structure activity relationship, structure-based drug design
  • Virtual screening. Structure based pharmacophore. Screening of various chemical databases (Ex-Zinc database)

Immunoinformatics

  • Homology modeling of antibody. Tools--RosettaAntibody, PIGS, WAM
  • Prediction of Cytotoxic T Cell (MHC Class I) Epitopes- Antigen Processing in the MHC ClassI Pathway. Tools--NetCTL, SYFPEITHI, CTLPred, NetMHC server

ABOUT US

Ciencia life science is an integrated Life sciences research company offering various research training services and innovative solutions to the life sciences community. This company is started by PhD holders having proficient research background. During the course of the doctorate program, we observed the need for an integrated service provider for the fast growing life sciences and biotech companies and academics. Our motto is to provide good training in research by qualified professionals, integrated consulting and outsourcing of research.

We are the leading bioinformatics training organization located in Hyderabad. We provide high quality services and training in the area of bioinformatics, and cheminformatics for the past 3 years. We also offer advanced trainings, short courses, summer projects, Bio IT courses (bioperl, Python) in the field of bioinformatics and cheminformatics. The training is available for both classroom and online.

We also support researches by intelligently analyzing data from their projects, support in writing projects to submit to any funding agencies and publications. Data Analysis group will be able to help move your research project forward in a number of ways, and we have the experience to assist you at every step of the process. We support clinical trials and Pharma research by applying cheminformatics tools. We maintain the confidentiality of those who use our services. We are committed to improve your confidence by safeguarding your personal information. All the information you provide to us is securely maintained and is kept strictly confidential.


Advisory Board

Dr.Kaiser Jamil is a distinguished scientist in the fields of biotechnology, cancer biology, pharmacogenomics, and toxicogenomics. She serves as the Dean and Director of the School of Life Sciences at Jawaharlal Nehru Institute of Advanced Studies (JNIAS) and is also an Emeritus Research Scientist and Head of the Genetics Department at Bhagwan Mahavir Medical Research Centre, Hyderabad.

Previously, she held key positions at CSIR-IICT, CCMB, and Indo-American Cancer Institute. Dr. Jamil has published over 295 research papers, authored books, and supervised numerous Ph.D. students. She has received multiple awards, including the Lifetime Achievement Award and Fellowships from esteemed scientific societies. Her research focuses on cancer biomarkers, SNP analysis, drug-gene interactions, and environmental genotoxicity. Know More

Dr. Vishnu Vardhan Reddy Martha is a distinguished Professor in the Department of Otolaryngology-Head and Neck Surgery at Malla Reddy Institute of Medical Sciences, Hyderabad, India, and an Adjunct Professor at Drexel University College of Medicine, USA. With extensive experience in otolaryngology, he specializes in head and neck surgery, audiology, and voice disorders.

He has made significant contributions to medical research, focusing on syndromic and non- syndromic deafness, head and neck cancers, and laryngeal disorders. His work includes numerous publications, international collaborations, and mentorship of Ph.D. candidates. He has also served in leadership and advisory roles in prestigious institutions and medical committees worldwide. Know More

Dr. A. Uma is an Associate Professor at the Centre for Biotechnology, Institute of Science and Technology, Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University (JNTU), Hyderabad. She specializes in biochemistry, biotechnology, and biofuels research, with expertise in enzyme engineering, metabolic studies, and bioethanol production.

She has led multiple research projects funded by DST, DBT, AICTE, and CSIR, focusing on biofuel production, obesity-related metabolic studies, and molecular biomarker analysis. Dr. Uma has organized several scientific workshops and conferences and has contributed extensively to research with numerous publications and patents. Know More

Dr. Rambabu Gundla is a Professor in the Department of Chemistry at GITAM University, Hyderabad. He specializes in the discovery and development of biologically active compounds, including anti-inflammatory, antiviral, anticancer, and antidiabetic agents.

With a Ph.D. from Osmania University and postdoctoral research at the University of Southern California, he has over 106 publications, 16 patents, and extensive experience in computer-aided drug design, SAR, QSAR, molecular docking, and virtual screening. He has collaborated with top pharmaceutical companies and led multiple government-funded research projects. Know More

Dr. N. K. Katari is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Chemistry at GITAM Deemed to be University, Hyderabad. With over 16 years of research and 12 years of teaching experience, his expertise lies in analytical and organic chemistry, particularly in Quality by Design-based method development, risk analysis, and green analytical tools.

He has published extensively with 185 research papers, 20 patents, and multiple funded and consultancy projects. Dr. Katari has significant international research exposure, collaborating with institutions in the USA, South Africa, and China. He has also served as a scientific advisor, reviewer, and editorial board member for various reputed journals.

His research focuses on drug analysis, impurity profiling, chromatography, spectroscopy, and pharmaceutical formulations. He has guided numerous Ph.D. and M.Sc. students and has been recognized with prestigious awards, including a university gold medal and best researcher awards. Know More

Scientific Writings

In science, writing is the most important means of communicating research findings. In most cases, researchers report the results of their research activities in scientific journals in a rather standard scientific paper format. Scientific writing should be simple, clear and well-organized communication to transmit.

The first journal was published about 350 years ago, and the IMRAD (introduction, methods, results and discussion) organization of scientific papers has developed within about the past century. In olden days people could communicate orally but without written records to refer to knowledge was lost almost as quickly as it was found. The first scientific journals started in 1665, one is journals Dev Savans in France and the other one Philosophical transaction of the royal of society London in England, Began publication. As of 2014, there were nearly 35000 peer reviewed journals in science, technology and medicine. The number of scientific publications per year has been increasing exponentially.

The thought of preparing a piece of scientific writing can intimidate even the best writes. However establishing a suitable mindset and taking an appropriate approach can make the task manageable. Perhaps most basic, remember that you are writing to communicate not to impress. In scientific writings people should observe only the content, not the style.
